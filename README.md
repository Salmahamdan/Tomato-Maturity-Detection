# Tomato-Maturity-Detection
Exploring Deep Learning for Tomato Maturity Detection: A Journey through Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN), VGG16, and ResNet50

Embarking on a fascinating journey into the realm of computer vision, I recently delved into a project focused on Tomato Maturity Detection. Leveraging the power of deep learning, I explored three formidable architectures: Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN), VGG16, and ResNet50. Let's take a glance at the key chapters of this captivating adventure:

#1. Data Exploration and Preparation:
  - Unveiled the world of Tomato Maturity Detection through a diverse dataset.
  - Crafted a pandas DataFrame, wielding the paths to images and their corresponding labels.

#2. Visualization:*
  - Painted a canvas of Tomato-Maturity-Detection, showcasing a random subset with their respective labels.
 
*3. Model Crafting:*
  - *CNN:* Constructed a Convolutional Neural Network, unveiling its layers from convolution to dense.
  - *VGG16:* Enlisted the renowned VGG16 architecture and pre-trained on ImageNet.
  - *ResNet50:* Tapped into the power of ResNet50, featuring residual connections for deeper and more accurate learning.

*4. Model Training and Evaluation:*
  - Trained each model on a carefully split dataset, incorporating training, validation, and testing sets.
  - Monitored the evolution of accuracy and loss through epochs.

*5. Model Comparison and Selection:*
  - Pitted the models against each other in a battle of accuracy.
  - Declared the champion, the model with the highest validation accuracy.

*6. Model Deployment and Interaction:*
  - Preserved the victorious model for future endeavors.
  - Demonstrated its prowess on new, unseen images, unveiling the magic of real-world prediction.

This project was more than code and data; it was a journey into the diverse world of Tomato Maturity Detection. Each model, a unique character, contributed to the narrative of understanding and inclusivity.
